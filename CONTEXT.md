# Owlet records

Records of readings reported by an Owlet Dream Sock, used to review sleep and compare observations over time.

## Language

**Latest reading**:
The most recently recorded reading reported by Owlet, together with its age. It does not establish the baby's current condition.
_Avoid_: Live measurement

**Live**:
The view of the latest recorded readings and their freshness. The name describes the app destination, not an alarm or a guarantee of current conditions.

**Trends**:
Changes in recorded statistics across nights, viewed over the available history.
_Avoid_: Health score
